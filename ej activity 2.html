Traditional Menudo Recipe

Menudo is a hearty Mexican stew made with beef tripe, hominy, and a flavorful broth. It's often served with tortillas and enjoyed as a comforting meal. This recipe serves about 6-8 people and takes around 3-4 hours to prepare.

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs beef tripe (cleaned and cut into bite-sized pieces)
  • 1 lb beef feet or knuckles (for richness)
  • 1 large onion, quartered
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tbsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 can (15 oz) hominy, drained and rinsed
  • 4 cups beef broth or water
  • 2 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh cilantro, chopped (for garnish)
  • Lime wedges (for serving)
  • Tortillas or bolillos (for serving)

Instructions

  1. In a large pot, add the beef tripe, beef feet, onion, garlic, bay leaves, oregano, cumin, and enough beef broth or water to cover the ingredients. Bring to a boil over high heat.
  2. Reduce heat to low and simmer for 2-3 hours, or until the tripe is tender. Skim off any foam that rises to the surface.
  3. Remove the beef feet and bay leaves. Shred the meat from the feet if desired and return to the pot.
  4. Add the hominy and simmer for another 30 minutes to allow flavors to meld.
  5. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Stir in chopped cilantro just before serving.
  6. Serve hot with lime wedges, tortillas, and additional cilantro.

Note: Menudo is traditionally made with cow's stomach lining (tripe), which requires thorough cleaning. If you're new to this, ask your butcher for pre-cleaned tripe. Adjust spices to your preference for heat.
