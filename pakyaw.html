    Giniling Recipe

    • 2 2 tbsp of butter, divided
    • 1 1 medium red onion
    • 1 1tsp minced garlic
    • 1 3 medium chopped potatoes
    • 1 1 lb ground pork
    • 4 tomato sauce or spaghetti sauce to taste.
    • 2 Ground pepper to taste
    • 1 salt or fish sauce to taste
    • optional Green peas

    Instructions:

    1. Melt 1 tbsp of butter on your skillet.
    2. Cook the chopped potatoes until they are tender and slightly toasted set aside.
    3. Brown the ground pork Drain and set aside.
    4. Melt another tbsp of butter.
    5. Saute the minned garlic followed by the onion.
    6. Mix in the ground !
    7. add some fish sauce or salt and then are ground pepper according to your taste
    8. put a half cup of water if you want it to have sauce and let it simmer for 5 minutes
    9. Mix in the potatoes
    10. put some tomato sauce according to your taste
    11. put some green peas only if you want to again this is optional
    12. let it simmer for about 5 to 10 minutes that's it
